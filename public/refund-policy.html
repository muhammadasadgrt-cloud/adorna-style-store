Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We want you to love your Adorna Style pieces. If something isn't right, here's how returns and exchanges work.

Exchange Window

You may request an exchange within 3 days of receiving your order if:

  • The item arrived damaged or defective
  • You received the wrong item
  • The item is significantly different from what was shown on the website

Condition for Return

Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with tags attached. For hygiene reasons, earrings cannot be exchanged unless there is a manufacturing defect.

How to Request an Exchange

Contact us within 3 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item:

  • WhatsApp/Phone: +971 54 295 7698
  • Email: ab732099@gmail.com

Once approved, we'll arrange for the item to be picked up or ask you to send it back, and dispatch your replacement or store credit.

Refunds

Since most orders are paid via Cash on Delivery, approved returns are generally resolved as an exchange or store credit rather than a cash refund. For orders paid online via JazzCash or Easypaisa, refunds (when applicable) are returned to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

Who Pays Return Shipping

If the return is due to our error (wrong/damaged item), we cover the return shipping cost. If you simply changed your mind, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

💡 This is a general policy — exact return windows and conditions are at Adorna Style's discretion and may be updated. When in doubt, just message us — we're happy to help.
