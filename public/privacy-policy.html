Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Adorna Style ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you shop with us, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and delivery address
  • Your email address (if you choose to provide it)
  • Order details — items purchased, quantities, and total amount
  • Payment method selected (Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, or Easypaisa)

We do not collect or store your card numbers, JazzCash/Easypaisa PINs, or wallet passwords — payments are processed directly and securely by JazzCash/Easypaisa's own systems, not by us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order status via phone, email, or WhatsApp
  • To improve our products and shopping experience

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

Our shopping cart is stored in your browser (not on our servers) so your selected items stay saved between visits on the same device. Clearing your browser data will clear your cart.

Data Sharing

We share your delivery details only with our courier/delivery partner to fulfill your order, and with JazzCash/Easypaisa when you choose those payment methods (only the information required to process the payment).

Your Rights

You can ask us to view, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us below.
